# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and maintainers, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, or experience level.

## Standards

- Treat everyone with respect and kindness.
- Welcome constructive feedback and diverse viewpoints.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Violations or concerns may be reported directly to the project maintainers.
